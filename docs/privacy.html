1. What stays on your device

All of the App’s core features run locally on your phone, without our servers (we do not operate any servers for the App):

  • Scanning and recovery. The App reads your device storage, the Android system trash and manufacturer recycle bins to find deleted or hidden files and show them to you. File contents, names and paths are processed only on the device.
  • Smart trash and Deletion watchdog. To tell you when files were deleted and how long they can still be restored, the App keeps a local index of media identifiers, sizes and dates in a database on your phone.
  • Encrypted vault. Files you move to the vault are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is kept in Android’s secure storage (Keystore) and is never sent to us.
  • Biometric unlock. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android. The App only receives a “success” or “failure” answer and never has access to your biometric data.
  • Cleaning tools. Junk files, large files and duplicate detection (which compares file fingerprints/hashes) all run locally.
  • Settings and notifications. Your preferences (language, notifications, watchdog and so on) are stored on the device. Reminder notifications are generated locally.

Android’s automatic cloud backup is disabled for the App, so this local data is not copied to your Google account backup.

4. Advertising and consent

The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Subscribers do not see ads.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App uses Google’s certified consent platform (User Messaging Platform) to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where this choice applies).
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads, depending on your device).

5. Subscriptions

Trash Vault Pro is sold through Google Play. We never see your name, email or card number. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt so the App can confirm that your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

6. What we never collect

  • The contents, names or paths of your photos, videos, audio, documents or messages
  • Files in your vault, or the vault’s encryption key
  • Biometric data
  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • An account — the App has no sign-in

7. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and the related use of your advertising ID. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Legitimate interests — for crash reporting, basic app statistics, remote configuration and the delivery of non-personalised ads, so we can keep the App stable, improve it and keep a free version available.
  • Performance of a contract — for processing and verifying your subscription.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data (database, index, vault, settings) stays on your phone until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes it. Please move any files out of the vault before uninstalling, because vault files cannot be decrypted without the key that is removed with the App.
  • Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept according to the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings (no longer than 14 months for user-level data).
  • AdMob, Google Play and RevenueCat keep data according to their own policies and legal obligations (for example, purchase records required for tax purposes).

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account or your identity, most of these requests are handled directly in the App, in Android Settings or with Google. For anything else, email us at hamid.dev2030@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy: the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

10. Security

Vault files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and the key is protected by the Android Keystore. Data sent to the third-party services above travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we design the App to keep your files on your device in the first place.

11. International transfers
